GET COMFORTABLE

From download
to sundown.

The current preview is for Apple silicon Macs running macOS 14 or later. Intel builds are not included yet.

  1. 1

    Make it at home.

    Download and unzip SolarDial. Move SolarDial.app to your Applications folder.

  2. 2

    Give it the screen.

    Quit other screen-warming apps and turn off Night Shift to avoid competing colour adjustments.

  3. 3

    Find your rhythm.

    Open Settings from the menu bar. Set your wake time (or custom times by day), sleep length, and favourite evening colours.

About this preview

Version 0.2.0 is ad-hoc signed and not notarized by Apple. If macOS blocks it, attempt to open the app, then use System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway if you trust this build. Keep Gatekeeper enabled. You can skip location permission and use the time-zone estimate.

A few things to know.

Can I customize the colours?

Yes. Day, sunset, and bedtime each have their own colour-temperature setting. Movie Mode has a separate colour and duration. Optional bedtime dimming follows the bedtime transition.

Does it work with external displays?

SolarDial applies colour adjustments per display and supports per-display full-screen pauses. Bedtime dimming also works through display colour tables, so it doesn’t require an external monitor’s brightness controls.

Do I need to share my location?

No. If you skip Location Services, SolarDial uses an automatic time-zone estimate. You can also choose a time zone manually from a list of regions and cities. The app never sends your location over the network.

Is SolarDial affiliated with f.lux?

No. SolarDial is an independently implemented app inspired by the idea of adapting screen colour to the day. It is not made or endorsed by f.lux.

What happens when I quit?

Normal display colour is restored. Closing the Settings window keeps SolarDial running in the menu bar; choose Quit SolarDial to stop it.
